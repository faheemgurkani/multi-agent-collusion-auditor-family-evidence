# Logistics talk: key pointers

Source: "Sprint Logistics: how the weekend works", Kamil Alaa (Apart), Fri Oct 23, 12:15pm ET. Transcript and chat are in [`../misc./transcripts/Logistics/`](../misc./transcripts/Logistics/). [Zoom recording](https://us06web.zoom.us/rec/share/s0vpj0hYBL1l9lvkEvI5SsPDc3M6AOUk8CSKRnxccwoKU_RNcGmZ-UO3OQ9noHXj.6e4esUiVAiiOKwse).

Only points that add to, or sharpen, what's on the website are listed here.

## Scope and rules
- **Tracks are inspiration only.** Spanning several tracks is fine, and there's no hard rule.
- **The project must be about AI collusion.** General AI-safety projects are **disqualified**, because the judges are experts in this area. A collusion angle on cyber attacks is fine if it's relevant.
- A **Dolos-1.5B-only project is fine.**
- **Only weekend work is judged.** Disclose prior work explicitly: "if your work is online, I will find it", and undisclosed prior work leads to disqualification. Pre-existing checkpoints or code are OK if declared. New runs done during the sprint count as sprint work.
- Max **2 projects per person**. The advice is to focus on **one**: a 5-person team putting 3 days into a single project can log about 200 person-hours, and that's your competition.
- Team size: no hard cap, ~5 suggested; "teams that do best are between **1 and 3** people", and many winners are solo. Teams can change mid-event. **No team registration**: just list members in the submission form.

## The report (the only thing judged)
- PDF from the Guidelines-tab template. **Hard cap of 8 pages** for the main body (references and appendices excluded). "Paper is 5 pages, appendices 50" is OK: put tables, extra stats and data in appendices.
- **Judges score only the PDF.** Optional extras (code, video, slides, image) are *not* scored, though judges *may* look at code that's tightly linked to the report.
- Three criteria: **Impact & Innovation**, **Execution Quality**, **Presentation & Clarity**. "A judge cannot give you a good score if they don't understand your project."
- **Include the Limitations and Dual-Use section.** The topic is sensitive, so flag any possible nefarious uses.
- Spelling and grammar mistakes are fine. Clarity, innovation and impact are what count.
- **AI use:** allowed, and there are no AI detectors. But a one-prompt "Claude, write the report" won't work: judges can decline to score anything they can't follow, with no repercussions for them. (A rule against AI-heavy reports was removed this sprint; it's now up to the judge.) Have someone read the paper before submitting.
- Look at past winners: [apartresearch.com/sprints](https://apartresearch.com/sprints) → past sprint → Overview tab. The last first-place winner was ~8 pages with a table, plus extra pages of stats and references (saved in [examples/](examples/)).

## Submission mechanics
- "Submit your project" button under "Sign up" on the sprint page. After submitting, a **grant-making form** appears. **Fill it in**, since the best projects are sent to grant makers.
- **Deadline:** Sun Oct 25, 11:59 PM AoE (UTC−12) = **Mon Oct 26, 11:59 AM UTC**. No extensions ("maybe an hour or two of grace, but not today").
- **Fixing things:** resubmitting the form with the same title updates *form details only*. **The PDF is not replaced.** For a new PDF, make a new submission with a slightly changed title (safest) or email it, **on deadline day at the latest**. Projects go to judges a few hours after the deadline.
  - ⚠️ The hub document said "resubmitting with the same title replaces the old version". Kamil said this is **wrong / outdated**.
- Report must be public on the website to be judged. Submissions can take up to 12h to appear.

## Results and after
- Results in **2–3 weeks, maybe up to a month** (74 judges onboarded). The website says 1–2 weeks after the judging deadline.
- Roughly **80–100 best projects** are invited to the next step (Studio → Fellowship). You don't have to win to be invited, just "do good". The fellowship is **remote, funded** (stipend, compute, APIs).

## Practical
- **Modal: $50 compute per person.** A card is required at signup (not charged).
- Questions: Discord **#help-desk, tag @Support** (Kamil monitors it). Don't DM. Team finding: Discord **projects-and-teams** forum.
- Talk recordings: Discord announcements + Schedule tab. Zoom recordings include transcripts.
- **Pre-sprint survey** (per sprint): https://apartresearch.notion.site/2b0fcfd1de9d8080ac3ac4c41a78c8f8, which marks you as an active participant.
- **Refer a friend:** if a referred friend (on a different team) wins, you get the same reward.
- "Competition is fierce… some of the projects are very well polished."
