## LLM Usage Statement

We used Claude (Anthropic) as an AI coding and writing assistant throughout. It helped survey and summarise the literature, draft the pre-registration, write the experiment and analysis code, monitor runs, and draft sections of this report. The research question, design decisions and final text are the author's responsibility. Every number in the report was regenerated from raw logs by the released scripts and checked against them. Every reference was checked against the paper's PDF (title, authors, arXiv id). During verification we found and corrected one transposed results table (the E2 recognition probe) before writing. The LLMs studied in the experiments (Gemma 4, GPT-OSS-120B, Nemotron 3 Super, and DeepSeek V4 Flash as the summariser) are research subjects, not authoring tools.
